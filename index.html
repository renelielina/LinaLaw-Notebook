Backup & restore

Everything in this app is stored only on this phone, in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. If the phone is lost, reset, or the browser data is cleared, the records are gone unless you have a backup file.

Save the backup file to Google Drive or email it to yourself. Do this at least once a week.
Restoring adds everything from the backup. Entries already here that are also in the backup are replaced with the backup's version.

Good habits

  • Back up every Friday, and before changing or resetting your phone.
  • To move to a new phone, open the app there and restore your latest backup.
  • On iPhone, open the app from its home-screen icon so Safari keeps the data.
  • Keep the phone locked with a PIN. Client records and IDs are stored here.
